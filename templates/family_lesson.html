Family Relationship Signs

In this lesson, you will learn the basic signs used to talk about family members in Nigerian Sign Language (NSL). These signs are very useful for everyday conversation.

1. Male / boy

Description: Grasp the imaginary brim of a hat with four fingers and thumb.

2. Female/ girl

Description: Move the inside of the thumb of the right “A” down along the right cheek toward the chin.

3. Father/ Daddy/ Dad

Description: Using the “FIVE” hand, place the thumb tip against the forehead.

4. Mother/ mom/ mommy/ mum

Description: Using the “FIVE” hand, place the thumb tip against the chin.

5. Son

Description: Sign for son.

6. Daughter

Description: Sign “FEMALE”; then place the right hand, palm up, in the crook of the left arm..

7. Brother

Description: Sign “MALE” and “SAME” (place both index fi ngers side by side, pointing to the front).

8. Sister

Description: Sign “FEMALE” and “SAME” (place both index fingers side by side, pointing to the front).

9. Gentleman

Description: later.

10. Lady

Description: later.

11. Grandmother

Description: Place the thumb tip of the “FIVE” hand at the chin and swing it away in a double movement.

12. Grandfather

Description: Place the thumb tip of the “FIVE” hand at the forehead and swing it away in a double movement.

13. Marry

Description: Clasp the hands, with right hand on top..

14. Husband

Description: Sign “MALE”; then clasp hands, right hand on top, as in “MARRIAGE.”

15. Wife

Description: Sign “FEMALE”; then clasp hands as in “MARRIAGE.”

16. Engagement

Description: Place the right “E” on the fourth finger of the left hand

Origin: The engagement ring is placed on the finger

17. Wedding

Description: With palms facing the body and fingers pointing forward, bring the right hand into the left between the thumb and index finger; left hand grasps right.

18. Divorce

Description: Place the “D” hands before you, palms slightly inward, and give them a quick twist outward and away from each other ending with palm side out.

20. Stepfather
