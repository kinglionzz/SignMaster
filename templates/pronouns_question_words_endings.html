Pronouns, Question Words, and Endings

1. I

Description: The “I” hand is placed at the chest.

2. Me

Description: Point the right index finger at yourself.

3. We

Description: Place the index finger at the right shoulder and circle it forward and around until it touches the left shoulder.

4. Us

Description: Place the "U" hand at the right shoulder and circle it forward and around until it touches the left shoulder.

5. You

Description: Point the index finger out. For the plural, point the index finger out and move from left to right.

6. They

Description: #

7. Them

Description: #

8. These, Those

Description: #

9. This

Description: Place the tip of the right index finger into the left open palm.

10. My, Mine

Description: Place the open palm on the chest. Origin: showing possession by holding an object against the chest

11. His

Description: #

12. Her

Description: #

13. Their, Theirs

Description: #

14. Our

Description: Place the right hand, slightly cupped, at the right shoulder with thumb side against the body; circle it around until the little-finger side touches the left shoulder.

15. Self, Myself

Description: Strike the “A” hand, palm side facing left, against the chest several times.

16. Ourselves

Description: #

17. Yourself

Description: #

18. Himself

Description: #

19. Herself

Description: later.

20. Themselves

Description: later.

21. Itself

Description: later.

22. Each, Every

Description: Hold up the left “A” and use the inside of the thumb of the right “A” to make downward strokes on the back of the left thumb. (Each is done once, and every twice.)

23. Someone

Description: #

24. Somebody

Description: #

25. Something

Description: #

26. Any

Description: Place the “A” hand before the body and draw it to the right while moving it up and down from the wrists so that the thumb points first up and then down.

27. Anyone

Description: Sign “ANY” and “ONE.”

28. Anybody

Description: Sign “ANY” and “BODY.”

29. Anything

Description: Sign “ANY” and “THING.”

30. Everybody

Description: Sign “EACH” and “BODY.”

31. Everyone

Description: Sign “EACH” and “ONE.”

32. Other

Description: Move the “O” hand slightly up and to the right, turning the thumb up and over toward the right.

33. Another

Description: Move the “A” hand slightly up and to the right, turning the thumb up and over toward the right.

34. Fellowship

Description: Circle the right “A” which is pointing down in a counterclockwise motion around the thumb of the left “A” which is pointing up and moving in a counterclockwise motion.

35. That

Description: Place the right “Y” on the left palm.

36. Who

Description: The index finger draws a circle (leftward) in front of the lips.

37. Which

Description: Place both “A” hands before you with palms facing each other and raise and lower them alternately..

38. How

Description: Place the curved hands back to back with fingers pointing down; turn hands away from, then toward, yourself in this position until fingers point up.

39. What

Description: Draw the tip of the right index (which is facing self) downward across the left open palm. Or hold both hands out palms up and move your palms in and out. Some prefer to simply fingerspell “what.”

40. Why

Description: Touch the fingertips to the forehead and draw them away, forming a “Y” (palm facing self). “WHY” is often fingerspelled.

41. When

Description: left index is held up facing you; right index faces out and draws a circle in front of the left index and comes to rest on the tip of the left index.

42. Where

Description: Hold up the right index finger and shake the hand back and forth quickly from left to right.

43. A, AN, THE

Description: A, AN, THE—As a rule, articles are omitted in the language of signs as used by deaf adults. However, in manual English systems, they are included for educational purposes. In these cases, they are made as follows:
A—Move the right “A” slightly to the right;
AN—fingerspell;
THE—Turn the “T” from a palm-left to a palm
forward position.

44. Person

Description: #

45. Person Ending

Description: Both open hands facing each other are brought down in front of the body. This sign is an ending only and is used following verbs, occupations, and locations.

Usage: teach—teacher; America—American; act—actor; law—lawyer; south—southerner; prophesy— prophet.

Note: When the word ending designates a specific person, use that sign, as: salesman = “sales” + “man.”

46. Negative

Description: #

47. -Ment

Description: Place the side of the right “M” against the left palm (which has fingertips pointing up); move “M” downward.

48. -Ness

Description: Place the side of the right “N” against the left palm (which has fingertips pointing up); move “N” downward.

49. Pre

Description: Place the back of the right “P” against the left palm and move the “P” away from the palm toward self.

50. Post

Description: Place the palm side of the right “P” against the back of the left hand and move the “P” away from the hand.
